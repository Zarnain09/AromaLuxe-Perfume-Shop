About AromaLuxe

AromaLuxe was created to bring the experience of discovering a signature fragrance online — without losing the elegance of an in-store perfume counter.

We curate fragrances across Men's, Women's, and Unisex collections, spanning woody, floral, fresh, oriental, and gourmand families, so every customer can find a scent that tells their story.

This website was built as an academic e-commerce project demonstrating a full online shopping flow — from browsing and filtering products to cart, checkout, reviews, and account management.
